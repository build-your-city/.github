# Build Your City
**Use your skills. Serve your city.**

Build Your City is a network of city communities where builders use their skills to serve local nonprofits. Developers, designers, content creators, project managers, marketers, and more. All builders are welcome.

**Website**: [build-your-city.org](https://build-your-city.org)

## Our cities
- **Build Columbus** (Columbus, Ohio): [buildcolumbus.org](https://buildcolumbus.org)

## What we do
- **Meetup**: Once a month in each city. Talks, workshops, and time to connect with other builders.
- **Serve**: Volunteer with local nonprofits and see where your skills are needed.
- **Hack**: A day to work on real problems facing local nonprofits. Hear from the people doing the work, pick a challenge, and present your idea.
